02 / Research

Inside the work.

My current research asks how intelligent systems represent what is implied, remembered, and actionable.

01

Computational argumentation

Built an end-to-end SPORC pipeline for stance, turn type, conversation moves, entailment, assumptions, and Gricean maxim violations with GPU-backed LLM inference. Designed analyses of relevance bridges, the assumption “iceberg,” implicature flow, processing load, repair cascades, and social-power dynamics.

02

StateBridge

Designed an object-centric, uncertainty-aware belief-state bottleneck for preserving task-relevant facts after they leave an embodied agent’s field of view, with a TEACh EDH protocol spanning hidden-state recovery, interventions, next-action prediction, and closed-loop execution.

03

Compositional robot manipulation

Developed a factorized ManiSkill 3 research plan comparing behavior cloning, ACT, Diffusion Policy, and object-centric ACT across isolated OOD factors, with a primary controlled study of failure-guided demonstration collection.

04

Reproducible research systems

Built local and Slurm workflows with patch-safe merges, data-leakage controls, robustness specifications, evaluation summaries, and publication-ready visualizations.
